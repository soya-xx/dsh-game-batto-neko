## BattoNeko

Soya is a cat-eared swordswoman who wakes in the Misty Forest.
Follow the forest paths down into the glowing underground city, meet the hamsters of Hamster Town, and fight the bosses that guard the way.

- **Hand-drawn pixel art** with layered forests, glowing caves and lantern-lit towns
- **Sword combat with weight**: charged strikes, dashes, arts, and weapons you collect along the way
- **Bosses with a choice**: after some victories you decide whether to spare the boss or finish it
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch controls; English and Chinese

**Early access:** this store version is playable through Hamster Town. Later chapters are still being made.

*The game sends anonymous play data (rooms visited, deaths, boss fights) to help us tune it. No account or personal information is collected.*
